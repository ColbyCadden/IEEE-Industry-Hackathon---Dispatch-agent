**RANKED SHORTLIST**

1. **CalTRACS** — Calgary intersection turning movement counts, segment volumes, speeds, vehicle classification
   - Signals: City-owned, explicitly licensed under Open Government Licence – City of Calgary (permissive, worldwide, perpetual, non-exclusive, commercial OK), downloadable Excel/PDF, permanent monitoring stations + manual counts, 40+ years history, correlated datasets (bike/pedestrian/truck data also available)
   - Watch out: City of Calgary roads only; provincial highways excluded (Alberta Transportation data separate)
   - Source: https://trafficcounts.calgary.ca/ and https://data.calgary.ca/Transportation-Transit/Traffic-Volumes-for-2024/cauu-7hnw

2. **Alberta Transportation Highway Traffic Counts** — Provincial highways and 2,700 highway intersections statewide
   - Signals: Open Government Licence – Alberta, 390+ permanent automated recorder sites, turning movement counts, AADT data (annual), downloadable Excel
   - Watch out: Provincial highways only; does not cover city streets; video logs mentioned but access/format not detailed
   - Source: https://www.alberta.ca/highway-traffic-counts and https://open.alberta.ca/dataset?tags=traffic+counts

3. **Open Calgary Data Portal** — Aggregated datasets (2018–2024 traffic volumes, Socrata-backed)
   - Signals: Tabular data, OData endpoints (Excel/Tableau connectors), public domain, no paywall
   - Watch out: Not real-time; lags ~1 year behind collection; video not available
   - Source: https://data.calgary.ca/Transportation-Transit/
